# Positioning statements — Side Glance

> ⚠️ IN PROGRESS — nine copy-only Figma candidates created for review; no variant is yet the canonical homepage message.

This draft uses a provisional behavioral ICP because the full Carol input files do not yet exist. It targets an individual technical builder who runs multiple long-lived coding-agent sessions and has become the watcher of their own automation. The pricing strategy is undeclared, so the price-story gate was limited to internal consistency; none of these variants makes a premium or affordability claim.

## Minimal needs ladder

- One level down: polling terminal tabs, tmux panes, and worktrees for lifecycle state.
- Side Glance's level: turning working, waiting, ready, and failed lifecycle events into ambient terminal and tmux color without ingesting session content.
- One level up: keep more parallel coding work moving toward review while attention stays on the next decision or the life currently in the foreground.

## Source diagnosis

Original headline: “Your terminal knows when it needs you.”

Original support: “Thermal status for coding agents. Without touching your work.”

“Thermal status” exposes an internal visual metaphor instead of the customer’s outcome. “Without touching your work” is vague about the actual privacy boundary. The variants below replace those failures with either the one-level-up promise or a concrete product trade-off.

## Rude-QA pressure test

- “Life in the foreground” is too broad as a standalone strategic claim. It survives only as the brand-platform variant because its supporting copy narrows it to background agents and visible lifecycle state.
- “More agents. Not more watching.” and “No new dashboard. Your terminal.” pass the Opposite Test: command centers and active monitoring are real competing strategies.
- No variant implies mobile alerts, remote notification, autonomous intervention, transcript understanding, or guaranteed recovery after terminal failure.
- No benchmark, customer story, number, or unshipped capability was invented.
- The copy deliberately targets the repeated behavior—parallel long-running agent sessions—not a founder title, age, race, or other demographic proxy.
