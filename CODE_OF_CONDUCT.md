# Code of Conduct

Side Glance contributors and maintainers are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate in every project space. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, and a proportionate path to repair.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/AndrewUlloa). Do not use a public issue for a sensitive report.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
